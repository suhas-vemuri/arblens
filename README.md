\# arblens



\*\*Interactive options-market integrity and static-arbitrage research platform\*\*



\[Launch the Live ArbLens Demo](https://arblens.streamlit.app)



ArbLens scans option chains across multiple tickers and expirations, validates market data, removes weak or unreliable contracts, applies static no-arbitrage tests, evaluates findings using executable bid and ask prices, subtracts estimated transaction costs, and ranks the remaining results.



> Educational research project only. ArbLens does not place trades or provide investment advice.
